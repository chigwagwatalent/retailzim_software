Instructions

Teach users how to use the platform.

The site now has a product education path so shop owners understand the system before they register.

1Register your shopCreate the shop account, choose a plan, and set up users for owners, managers, and cashiers.
2Import productsUpload Excel products with prices, quantities, categories, and branch stock.
3Install POS appsDownload the Windows installer for tills and use the mobile app for floor selling.
4Open shift and sellCashiers open a shift, scan or search products, take payment, and print receipts.
5Close shift and reviewClose the day with reports, cash checks, stock movement, and cloud dashboard history.
