How it works

From setup to first sale in a simple shop workflow.

Retail Zim is designed so owners understand the system before they register and cashiers understand what to do at the till.

Steps

Follow the flow.

Use this page as a simple training guide for shop owners, managers, and cashiers.

1Register your shopCreate your Retail Zim account and set up users for owners, managers, and cashiers.
2Add your productsImport products from Excel or add them manually with prices, quantities, and categories.
3Install your appsUse Windows POS at the till and mobile POS for sales or stock checks from a phone.
4Open a shift and sellCashiers search or scan products, take payment, print receipts, and keep the queue moving.
5Close and reviewClose the shift, check totals, review reports, and understand what happened in your shop.
