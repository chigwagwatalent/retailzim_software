FULL DESCRIPTION (4,000 chars max)

RetailZW POS is a point-of-sale and retail management system designed specifically for shops in Zimbabwe. Run your business faster, smarter, and even offline.

KEY FEATURES:

🛒 SALES & CHECKOUT
- Barcode scanner support for fast item lookup
- Quick manual item search
- Process multiple items per transaction
- Apply discounts at line or basket level
- Print or email receipts

💰 MULTI-CURRENCY PAYMENTS
- Accept USD cash and ZWG cash
- EcoCash mobile money integration
- Zipit bank transfers
- Card payments
- Split payments across methods

📦 STOCK MANAGEMENT
- Real-time inventory tracking
- Low-stock alerts and notifications
- Bulk stock import via CSV
- Stock take / stockcount support
- Supplier and purchase order management

📊 REPORTING & ANALYTICS
- Daily, weekly, and monthly sales summaries
- Best-selling products
- Cashier performance reports
- Export reports to PDF or Excel

👥 STAFF & ACCESS CONTROL
- Multi-cashier support
- Role-based permissions (Owner, Manager, Cashier)
- Per-cashier shift and sales tracking

🔌 OFFLINE FIRST
- Full POS functionality without internet
- Automatic sync when connection is restored
- No data loss during outages

🔗 CLOUD SYNC
- Real-time sync with RetailZW web dashboard
- Manage your shop remotely from admin.retailzw.co.zw
- Multi-branch support on Growth and Enterprise plans

PRICING:
- Starter: $20/month (1 device, core POS features)
- Growth: $40/month (3 devices, advanced reporting)
- Enterprise: $100/month (unlimited devices, API access, priority support)

Operated by Charnodia Technologies (Pvt) Ltd, Zimbabwe.
